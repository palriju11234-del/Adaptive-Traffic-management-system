How It Works

Adaptive Algorithm:

green_time = 20 + (direction_demand / total_demand) × 40

The controller measures vehicle counts on approach lanes every cycle. The direction with more traffic receives proportionally more green time (20–60 seconds). Yellow (3s) and all-red (1s) safety intervals are always preserved.

Example:

NS = 8 vehicles, EW = 3 vehicles, Total = 11
NS green = 20 + (8/11) × 40 = 49s
EW green = 20 + (3/11) × 40 = 31s
